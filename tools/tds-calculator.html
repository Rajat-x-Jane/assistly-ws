What is this tool?

The TDS Calculator is a simple browser-based utility made for practical business planning. It is not designed to replace a professional consultation, but it helps you avoid a completely blank starting point. You can enter basic information, review a result, and then decide whether you need website, marketing, accounting, GST, or branding support.

Assistly WS is a new founder-led agency, so these tools are also part of the honest support approach. Instead of pushing you directly into a package, the goal is to help you understand the factors that affect cost, documents, timelines, and decisions.

How to use it step by step

  • Enter realistic details instead of ideal guesses.
  • Review the result as a planning estimate or working draft.
  • Note the assumptions that affect the number or output.
  • Open the related service or template if you need a next step.
  • Send the result on WhatsApp if you want a human review.

Common mistakes

  • Treating an estimate as a final quote without reviewing scope.
  • Ignoring documents, access, content, or approvals needed for execution.
  • Using old tax or compliance assumptions without checking current applicability.
  • Comparing only price and not timeline, support, clarity, and quality.
  • Not keeping a copy of the final details you want to share with a service provider.
