Who this service is for

Businesses deducting TDS, collecting TCS, paying vendors, or managing salary and professional payment compliance. This is especially useful when you want practical support, clear communication, and a scope that fits a small business budget.

What you get

  • Data review
  • Calculation support
  • Return preparation guidance
  • Correction support if needed

Required details or documents

  • TAN
  • Deductee details
  • PAN details
  • Challan details
  • Payment and deduction data

Step-by-step process

1

Understand

We discuss your goal, current status, budget, and timeline.

2

3

Prepare

I create the agreed work or coordinate the required support.

4

Review

You review, ask questions, and receive next-step guidance.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Wrong PAN details
  • Wrong section or rate
  • Late challan payment
  • Mismatch in challan data
