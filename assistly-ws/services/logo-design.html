Who this service is for

New businesses, local brands, freelancers, service providers, and early-stage product sellers. This is especially useful when you want practical support, clear communication, and a scope that fits a small business budget.

What you get

  • Logo concept direction
  • Color and typography guidance
  • Usable export files
  • Basic usage note

Required details or documents

  • Business name
  • Industry and audience
  • Style references
  • Preferred colors if any
