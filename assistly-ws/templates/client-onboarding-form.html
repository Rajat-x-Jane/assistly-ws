What is this template?

The Client Onboarding Form gives you a clear starting structure so you do not have to create the document from zero. It is designed for small business use, simple editing, and practical communication with customers, vendors, employees, or service providers.

When to use it

Use this template when you need a cleaner way to document business information, pricing, scope, records, or next steps. It can help reduce confusion, but final legal, tax, or compliance details should be reviewed for your exact situation.

Required fields

  • Business name
  • Contact details
  • Date and reference number
  • Customer or client details
  • Scope, items, or financial values
  • Terms, notes, or approval fields

How to use it step by step

  • Duplicate the template for your business.
  • Replace placeholder details with accurate information.
  • Check calculations, dates, and terms before sharing.
  • Save a final PDF copy for records when needed.
  • Ask for review if the document affects payment, tax, or legal terms.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving old placeholder text in the document.
  • Using unclear payment or delivery terms.
  • Forgetting invoice numbers, dates, GST details, or client information where needed.
  • Sharing editable files when a locked PDF would be safer.
