Who this service is for

Founders planning private limited company, LLP, partnership, proprietorship, or formal business setup. This is especially useful when you want practical support, clear communication, and a scope that fits a small business budget.

What you get

  • Structure guidance
  • Document checklist
  • Name planning support
  • Next compliance checklist

Required details or documents

  • PAN and Aadhaar
  • Address proof
  • Business name options
  • Registered office proof
  • Director or partner details

Step-by-step process

1

Understand

We discuss your goal, current status, budget, and timeline.

2

Collect

You share required content, access, details, or documents.

3

Prepare

I create the agreed work or coordinate the required support.

4

Review

You review, ask questions, and receive next-step guidance.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Choosing structure without tax and compliance clarity
  • Weak name options
  • Missing address proof
  • Ignoring post-registration compliance
