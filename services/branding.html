Who this service is for

New businesses, rebrands, local service providers, and founders who want consistent customer-facing material. This is especially useful when you want practical support, clear communication, and a scope that fits a small business budget.

What you get

  • Brand direction
  • Color palette
  • Typography guidance
  • Basic usage system

Required details or documents

  • Business goal
  • Audience
  • Competitor references
  • Current logo or materials if available

Step-by-step process

1

Understand

We discuss your goal, current status, budget, and timeline.

2

Collect

You share required content, access, details, or documents.

3

Prepare

I create the agreed work or coordinate the required support.

4

Review

You review, ask questions, and receive next-step guidance.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Skipping audience clarity
  • Using too many styles
  • No consistency across materials
  • Treating branding as only a logo
